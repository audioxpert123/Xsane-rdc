About XSANE

Community-made tools designed for Rust Mobile players.

Our purpose

Make useful in-game planning information easier to access, with a simple and mobile-friendly interface.

Data & accuracy

Raid values are being added and reviewed. Missing data is shown as unavailable rather than estimated. Game updates may change item names or values.
